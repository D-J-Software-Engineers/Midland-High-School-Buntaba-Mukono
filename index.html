Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for admission? +
You can apply online through our website's "Apply Online" portal or visit the school campus in Buntaba, Mukono for a physical application form.
What are the requirements for O'Level admission? +
Requirements include a PLE Result Slip, a recommendation letter from the previous school, and meeting the aggregate cutoff points set for the current year.
Does the school offer boarding facilities? +
Yes, we have well-built, secure, and comfortable dormitories for both boys and girls, with dedicated matrons and wardens.
Which subjects are offered in A'Level? +
We offer a wide range of Science and Art combinations, including PCM, BCM, HEG, MEG, and more, all supplemented by ICT and Sub-Math.
