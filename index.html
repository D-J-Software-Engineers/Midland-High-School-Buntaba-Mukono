Our Brief History

Midland High School was established to provide quality education in Buntaba, Mukono District. The school has grown to become one of the leading institutions in the region, offering both O'Level and A'Level programs.

Cultural Club

Technical Skills Club

At Midland High School, our Technical Skills Club offers hands-on training in carpentry, building, and other vocational skills for practical career development.

Agriculture Club

Midland Agriculture Club provides students with practical farming experience, teaching sustainable agriculture practices and food security awareness.
